# Rezzie brand system

## Position

Rezzie is the deliberate alternative to generic AI resume rewriting: it helps candidates make their real experience legible to the role, without overstating it. The feeling is an editorial career studio—clear-eyed, warm, precise, and human.

## Voice

- Lead with clarity over hype: “Stand out for the right reasons.”
- Use active, specific language: tailor, ground, review, make your case.
- Never imply a guaranteed interview, job, ATS score, or outcome.
- Do not use invented testimonials, client logos, performance statistics, or claims about accuracy that are not evidenced.

## Image direction

Use editorial still life, tactile paper, restrained desk tools, and natural warm light. The hero source is `apps/web/src/assets/rezzie-hero-editorial.png`; it was generated as an original project asset and intentionally contains no readable resume text. Avoid stock-office imagery, glowing AI brains, generic blue gradients, fake UI screenshots, and generated text.

## Product infographics

Use code-native diagrams rather than image files for the Rezzie Method: **Bring the brief → Find the fit → Review every word**. They remain responsive, accessible, and aligned with the live product rather than implying unverifiable results.
